      Each change is one Bluetooth write, at most one a second, so it steps rather than fades. It never writes during heat-up or while a curve's live drop is running, stops after 60 s or when the heat cycle ends, then puts your standby light back. Not during Wait for Heat, so your drop is never delayed.

        Studio V2 firmware (beta, Switch 2 and Switch Go). Custom firmware made for this app. It replaces the firmware on your device; you can go back to Dr. Dabber's firmware at any time. On the Switch 2 it stops the early ready buzz; on the Switch Go it rounds the heat target to the nearest degree, caps the top heat at 650 °F and won't start a heat under 6 % battery. While the beta is small, installing needs a Studio code.
